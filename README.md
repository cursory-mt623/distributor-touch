## 🚀 What Is distributor-touch?

distributor-touch is a powerful, user-friendly software that helps businesses manage their product distribution networks. Built specifically for the Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) industry, this tool simplifies how you track inventory, manage orders, and pay your sales partners automatically.

Whether you run a small distribution business or manage a large network of resellers, distributor-touch keeps everything organized in one place. No programming knowledge needed—just download, set up, and start working.

## 🎯 First Steps: Using Your New Tool

### 📊 Set Up Your Product Catalog
- Click **"Products"** in the top menu
- Select **"Add New Product"**
- Enter the product name, SKU number, and price
- Save your entry

Repeat this for every item you distribute. You can add hundreds of products easily.

### 🤝 Add Your Sales Partners
- Go to **"Partners"** or **"Affiliates"**
- Choose **"Add Partner"**
- Enter their name, contact information, and commission rate
- Click **"Save"**

Your partners now appear in the system, ready to start selling.

### 📈 Track Daily Orders
- Navigate to **"Orders"** 
- View all incoming orders from your various terminals
- Use the filter tools to sort by date, status, or product
- Approve or reject orders with one click

## 💡 Pro Tips for Best Results

### 🔄 Regular Backups
Click **"Settings"** then **"Backup"** to save a copy of your data once a week. Store the backup file on a USB drive or cloud storage for extra safety.

### 📱 Multi-User Access
Your staff can log in from different computers. Set up individual user accounts under **"Settings"** → **"Users"** with custom permissions for each team member.

### 🧮 Understanding Commission Reports
Each month, distributor-touch automatically generates payout reports. Go to **"Reports"** → **"Commissions"** to review totals before paying your partners.

## 🛡️ Built-In Compliance: What It Means For You

The 15 compliance guardrails are automatic checks that monitor your business rules. Here's a simple breakdown:

- **Price Consistency** – Ensures all terminals charge the same price for products
- **Inventory Accuracy** – Flags discrepancies before they become problems
- **Order Limits** – Prevents unrealistic bulk orders
- **Commission Caps** – Stops commission overpayments
- **Tax Calculations** – Automatically applies correct tax rates
- **License Tracking** – Keeps you updated on operational permits
- **Delivery Windows** – Validates shipping timeframes
- **Returns Management** – Properly handles product returns
- **Discount Validation** – Confirms discount eligibility
- **Volume Thresholds** – Alerts you on unusual order sizes
- **Product Expiry** – Tracks shelf life for perishable goods
- **Partner Verification** – Confirms active sales partners
- **Transaction Logging** – Records every business action
- **Data Integrity** – Protects against corrupted records
- **System Health** – Monitors software performance

These features work silently in the background, giving you peace of mind.
